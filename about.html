OUR MISSION

Less noise. More signal.

TechPulse is a student-friendly technology news concept built around clarity, accessibility and practical value. Instead of overwhelming readers with jargon, every story aims to answer three questions: what happened, why it matters and what you can do with the information.

Our coverage spans artificial intelligence, web development, cybersecurity and consumer technology.
